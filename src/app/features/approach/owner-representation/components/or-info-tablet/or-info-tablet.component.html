06 — Why It Matters

Why Owners Need
an Advocate.

A commercial construction project generates thousands of decisions, documents, and dollars in motion. Without an expert watching your interests, small oversights compound into budget overruns, schedule slips, and disputes. An owner’s representative turns complexity into control—giving you the information and leverage to decide with confidence.

Studies consistently show that projects with independent oversight finish closer to budget and schedule. 320 Grit brings that discipline to your project from day one.
