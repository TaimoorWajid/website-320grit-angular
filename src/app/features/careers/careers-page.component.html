Our Culture

Built on People.
Driven by Excellence.

At 320 Grit, we believe a positive culture is the foundation of every successful project. We’ve built a company where people enjoy coming to work, take pride in what they do, and are committed to supporting one another. We foster an environment built on trust, respect, collaboration, and open communication - because great projects are delivered by great teams.

While we maintain an approachable atmosphere, we’re serious about our work. We hold ourselves to a high standard, embrace accountability, and are committed to delivering quality results for every client, every time. We value strong relationships, continuous growth, and creating a workplace where hard work is recognized, ideas are welcomed, and success is shared. At 320 Grit, our culture is about more than the work we do, it’s about the people we do it with.
